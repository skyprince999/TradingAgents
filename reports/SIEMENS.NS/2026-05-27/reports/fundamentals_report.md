**Fundamental Analysis Report: Siemens Limited (SIEMENS.NS)**
**Date:** May 27, 2026

### **1. Executive Summary**
Siemens Limited (India) is a leading technology company focused on industry, infrastructure, transport, and electrical power transmission and distribution. As of May 2026, the company commands a significant market capitalization of approximately ₹1.38 trillion. While the company maintains a robust balance sheet with exceptional liquidity, its valuation (P/E ~81.7) reflects high growth expectations. Recent financial statements indicate a period of transition, likely involving the divestment or restructuring of business segments, as evidenced by significant "Net Income from Discontinued Operations" and large-scale shifts in investing and financing cash flows.

---

### **2. Financial Performance & Profitability**
*   **Revenue Trends:** Quarterly revenue shows volatility but remains strong. For the quarter ending December 31, 2025, revenue was ₹38.31 billion, a decrease from the September peak of ₹51.71 billion.
*   **Net Income:** There was a notable decline in Net Income for the quarter ending Dec 2025 (₹2.77 billion) compared to the same quarter in the previous year (₹6.14 billion). This is largely due to the absence of the large "Discontinued Operations" income (₹2.51 billion in Dec 2024) that bolstered previous figures.
*   **Margins:**
    *   **Operating Margin:** ~10.0%
    *   **Profit Margin:** ~9.2%
    *   **Gross Profit:** Remained healthy at ₹11.15 billion for the latest quarter, suggesting stable core pricing power despite revenue fluctuations.

---

### **3. Balance Sheet Strength**
*   **Liquidity:** Siemens exhibits superior liquidity with a **Current Ratio of 2.57**. The company holds ₹69.86 billion in Cash and Short-term Investments.
*   **Capital Structure:** The balance sheet is exceptionally lean regarding debt. Total Debt stands at only ₹1.45 billion against a Common Stock Equity of ₹132.28 billion. The "Debt to Equity" ratio is negligible (<0.02 based on financial statements), providing significant "dry powder" for future expansions or acquisitions.
*   **Asset Quality:** Total assets stood at ₹199.89 billion as of Sept 2025. The company successfully reduced its liability profile from ₹100.42 billion to ₹67.51 billion over the last fiscal year.

---

### **4. Cash Flow Insights**
*   **Restructuring Signals:** The 2025 annual cash flow statement reveals massive activity:
    *   **Investing Cash Flow:** Inflow of **₹31.21 billion**, primarily driven by the sale of investments/business units.
    *   **Financing Cash Flow:** Outflow of **₹31.86 billion**, likely representing a return of capital to the parent company or shareholders following the aforementioned divestments.
*   **Operating Cash Flow (OCF):** OCF dropped to ₹3.75 billion in 2025 from ₹16.55 billion in 2024. Traders should monitor if this is a temporary result of the business separation or a trend in core operating efficiency.

---

### **5. Market Valuation & Trading Metrics**
*   **P/E Ratio:** The TTM P/E of **81.76** is significantly higher than historical industry averages, suggesting the market is pricing in a "growth premium" or valuing the company as a pure-play high-margin industrial tech firm following its restructuring.
*   **Price Action:** The stock is trading near its 50-day average (₹3480) and above its 200-day average (₹3233), indicating a sustained bullish trend.
*   **Dividend Yield:** Low at **0.39%**, as the company prioritizes reinvestment and restructuring over immediate yield.

---

### **Actionable Insights for Traders**
1.  **Valuation Caution:** The high P/E ratio leaves little room for error. Any earnings miss or slowdown in the Indian infrastructure/industrial sector could lead to significant price corrections.
2.  **Post-Restructuring Focus:** With the separation of certain operations (likely energy-related), investors should focus on the "Continuing Operations" growth. The core business remains highly profitable with strong gross margins.
3.  **Infrastructure Play:** Siemens is a primary beneficiary of India's capital expenditure (CapEx) cycle. Traders should correlate long positions with government announcements regarding power grids, metro rail projects, and industrial automation.
4.  **Cash-Rich Position:** The massive cash reserves (₹69B+) provide a safety net and potential for inorganic growth (M&A), which could be a catalyst for the next leg of stock appreciation.
