### **Company Overview**
State Bank of India (SBI) is India's largest public sector bank, providing a wide range of financial services including personal banking, corporate banking, and wealth management. As a systemic pillar of the Indian economy, SBI benefits from extensive reach and sovereign backing, making it a bellwether for the country's financial health.

### **Financial Performance Analysis**

#### **1. Revenue and Profitability Trends**
SBI has demonstrated robust and consistent growth over the last four fiscal years:
- **Total Revenue:** Grew from ₹2.70 trillion in 2023 to ₹3.95 trillion in 2026, representing a steady upward trajectory in operational scale.
- **Net Income:** Increased significantly from ₹556.48 billion in 2023 to ₹832.99 billion in 2026. This reflects a Compounded Annual Growth Rate (CAGR) of approximately 14.4% in net profits.
- **Net Interest Income (NII):** NII stood at ~₹2.00 trillion in 2026, up from ₹1.61 trillion in 2023, showcasing the bank's ability to maintain healthy margins despite fluctuating interest rate environments.

#### **2. Earnings and Valuation**
- **Earnings Per Share (EPS):** Basic EPS has climbed from ₹62.35 (2023) to ₹91.16 (2026). With a forward EPS estimate of ₹102.08, the growth momentum is expected to persist.
- **Price-to-Earnings (P/E) Ratio:** The current TTM P/E of 10.62x and a Forward P/E of 9.48x are attractive compared to historical averages and private-sector peers.
- **PEG Ratio:** A PEG ratio of 0.53 strongly suggests that the stock is undervalued relative to its earnings growth potential (ratios below 1.0 are typically considered undervalued).
- **Price-to-Book (P/B) Ratio:** Standing at 1.51x with a book value of ₹641.36, the valuation remains reasonable for the market leader.

#### **3. Balance Sheet Strength**
- **Asset Growth:** Total assets expanded from ₹59.54 trillion in 2023 to ₹83.22 trillion in 2026, indicating aggressive yet managed expansion.
- **Equity:** Stockholders' equity has nearly doubled from ₹3.59 trillion in 2023 to ₹5.96 trillion in 2026, strengthening the bank's capital adequacy.
- **Return on Equity (ROE):** At 15.48%, SBI is delivering healthy returns to its shareholders, outperforming many of its public sector counterparts.

### **Market Sentiment and Technical Context**

- **Dividend:** A yield of 1.79% provides a steady income component for long-term investors.
